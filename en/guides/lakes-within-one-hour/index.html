Beautiful Lakes Within One Hour of Vienna

Where to swim within an hour of the city, what it costs, and how to get there by train.

7 min readUpdated September 2026

Vienna is a good city for swimming. You can reach an old arm of the Danube by U-Bahn, a river lido by regional train and a steppe lake that feels like the seaside in about an hour.

This guide lists places where you can be in the water within roughly an hour of leaving the city centre, with the cost of entry and the best way to get there without a car.

Sample content: travel times, prices and facilities are illustrative and not yet verified.

The places

1. Alte Donau & Donauinsel

Vienna 22 · Danube

Fifteen minutes by U-Bahn to free swimming, SUP hire and lakeside bars.

Tip · Go on a weekday afternoon. The free swimming spots along the Arbeiterstrandbadstraße get crowded at weekends.

2. Strombad Kritzendorf

Klosterneuburg · Danube

A historic Danube river lido with its own S-Bahn stop.

Tip · A river lido with a village feel. Swim near the shore, because the current in the main stream is strong.

3. Neusiedler See – Podersdorf

Burgenland · steppe lake

Shallow, warm steppe lake with wind sports and a flat cycle path all the way round.

Tip · The lake is shallow and warms up early in the season, but it is exposed. Bring shade.

4. Baden bei Wien

Lower Austria · spa town

Spa town by S-Bahn: thermal pools, a rose garden and the Helenental valley.

Tip · The thermal pools are a warm option on days that are too cool for a lake.
