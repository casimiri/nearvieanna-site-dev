Best Day Trips From Vienna by Train

Direct trains where possible and a full day at the other end: wine valleys, mountains and a spa town.

8 min readUpdated September 2026

A good day trip by train needs a direct or simple connection and enough to do when you arrive. Every trip here gets you back in Vienna for dinner.

We list wine valleys, a mountain railway and a spa town, sorted by travel time. Check the current timetable before you leave, especially at weekends and outside the main season.

Sample content: travel times, prices and facilities are illustrative and not yet verified.

The places

1. Dürnstein & the Wachau

Lower Austria · Danube valley

A UNESCO valley of vineyards and castle ruins, an hour by train from Vienna.

Tip · Combine the train with a boat or a bike along the Danube and return a different way.

2. Semmering

Lower Austria · UNESCO railway

Ski and sled slopes reached by a UNESCO mountain railway, no car needed.

3. Schneeberg

Puchberg · Lower Austria

Vienna's 2,000 m mountain, reachable by train, with a rack railway to the top.
