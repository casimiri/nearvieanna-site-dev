Best Hikes Near Vienna Without a Car

Every walk starts and ends at a station or bus stop, with travel time, cost and how hard it really is.

9 min readUpdated September 2026

Every walk in this guide starts and ends at a station or bus stop, so you never need to get back to a parked car. They are ordered by how long the trip from the centre of Vienna takes, starting with walks you can reach on a city ticket and ending with 2,000 m mountains that make a full day out.

Travel times, prices and difficulty on each card come from our database and follow the same rules on every page of the site. The notes below each place are our own tips.

Sample content: travel times, prices and facilities are illustrative and not yet verified.

The places

1. Kahlenberg & Nussberg

Vienna 19 · Wienerwald

Easy to reach without a car, free, and perfect for a half-day trip.

Tip · Walk downhill rather than up. You get the view first and finish at a Heuriger next to the tram.

2. Bisamberg vineyards

Vienna 21 · Langenzersdorf

Quiet vineyard hill with wide views. Free, dog-friendly, forty minutes door to door.

Tip · The quietest option on this list, with dry meadows on the south slope that are at their best in spring.

3. Lainzer Tiergarten

Vienna 13 · Wienerwald

Wild boar and old-growth forest inside the city limits, free and with easy paths.

Tip · Dogs are not allowed inside. The gates close at dusk, so start early in winter.

4. Myra Falls

Muggendorf · Piestingtal

Wooden walkways over a gorge of waterfalls. Short, shady and great with kids.

Tip · Wooden steps and walkways run right next to the water. Expect wet spray and slippery boards after rain.
