Hohe Wand

A skywalk, via ferratas and alpine meadows; good with kids on the plateau.

Overview

A limestone wall rising straight from the Vienna basin. Drive or hike up to the plateau for the skywalk viewing platform, easy meadow walks, an animal park and, for climbers, several via ferratas.

Things to do

  • Skywalk viewing platform
  • Animal park and playground
  • Via ferratas for experienced climbers
  • Plateau walk to the Kleine Kanzel

Questions

Can I get there without a car?

Yes, but the last part is a steep walk up; a car is much easier.
