Lobau

Floodplain forest and quiet backwaters inside Vienna, free and flat.

Overview

The Viennese part of the Donau-Auen National Park: meadows, old Danube arms and forest paths, popular with walkers and cyclists. Some backwaters are used for swimming in summer.

Things to do

  • Nature trails through the floodplain forest
  • Cycle the flat forest roads
  • Summer swimming in a backwater
  • National park information centre

Questions

Is it part of the national park?

Yes, the Lobau is the Viennese section of the Donau-Auen National Park; stay on marked paths.
