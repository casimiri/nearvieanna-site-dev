Überblick

Beethoven's summer town. Sulphur springs feed the thermal baths, the Doblhoffpark is famous for its roses, and the Helenental valley starts at the edge of town for a shady riverside walk.

Was du tun kannst

  • Thermal bath
  • Rose garden in Doblhoffpark
  • Walk the Helenental to Rauhenstein ruin
  • Kurpark concerts
